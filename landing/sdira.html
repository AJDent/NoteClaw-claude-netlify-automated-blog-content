The Rules You Can't Break

7 prohibited transactions that can blow up your IRA.

Break one and your IRA can be disqualified, meaning the entire account balance gets treated as a distribution. Taxes. Penalties. Instantly.

  • 01No self-dealing. You can't buy a property from, or sell one to, yourself, your spouse, kids, parents, or grandparents.
  • 02No personal use. Your IRA can't own a vacation home you stay in. Not even for one night.
  • 03No sweat equity. You can't personally paint, repair, or manage IRA-owned property. That's a "disqualified service."
  • 04No loans to yourself. Your IRA can't lend money to you, your spouse, or lineal family members.
  • 05All income back to the IRA. Rent checks, interest payments, and sale proceeds go straight back into the account, never your pocket.
  • 06All expenses paid by the IRA. If the property needs a roof, your IRA pays for it. Not you.
  • 07Watch out for UBIT/UDFI. If your IRA uses financing, part of the profit may trigger unrelated business income tax. Notes typically avoid this.
