The Problem

"Passive" real estate is anything but.

Rentals mean tenants, turnover, capex, and property managers who "forgot" to send the statement. Syndications mean 5-7 year lockups and hope. Notes mean you become the bank, and the bank always gets paid first.

🚽

Rentals: Not Actually Passive

Vacancies, evictions, roof replacements, property manager fees, city inspections. "Passive" income that eats your Saturday.

📉

Stocks: All Volatility, No Cash Flow

Your S&P index fund doesn't send you a check every month. And when the market drops 30%, so does your net worth.

🔒

Syndications: Locked Up for Years

Money sits illiquid for 5-7 years while a sponsor figures things out. No monthly cash. No control. Fingers crossed on the exit.

Head to Head

Rental property vs. mortgage notes.

Rental Property

The Landlord Grind

  • ✕Tenant screening, evictions, turnover
  • ✕Capex: roofs, HVAC, plumbing, appliances
  • ✕Property manager takes 8-10% of gross
  • ✕Vacancy risk between tenants
  • ✕City inspections, licensing, code compliance
  • ✕Liability exposure from tenant injuries
Mortgage Notes

Be the Bank

  • ✓Borrower is responsible for the property
  • ✓Zero capex. You own the debt, not the roof
  • ✓Licensed servicer handles collection
  • ✓Payments continue regardless of vacancy
  • ✓Bank gets paid before homeowner keeps anything
  • ✓Real property collateral behind every dollar
